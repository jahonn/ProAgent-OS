# Pager Severity

Severity sev1 means a complete outage or customer data exposure. Page the incident commander immediately, any hour.

Severity sev2 means degraded service, including an elevated error rate or a failed replica. Page the primary on-call during the business day and the next hour after.

Severity sev3 is a single-customer issue or a noisy alert with no customer impact. File a ticket and do not page.

The clock starts when the page is acknowledged.
